网络入侵检测数据中攻击样本远少于正常流量且类别间高度悬殊（UNSW-NB15 训练集不平衡比达
431:1），常规分类器会系统性漏检少数类攻击。本文不提出新算法，而是在统一实验协议下对
类别不平衡处理方法进行系统比较与轻量化改进：3 种模型（Logistic Regression、MLP、
XGBoost）× 4 种处理方法（无处理、类别权重、随机过采样、SMOTE）× 3 种子共 36 组主实验，
全部超参数先于结果冻结，防泄漏纪律贯穿全程；并引入验证集阈值校准与特征筛选轻量化两项
扩展对照，以及方法排序一致性 τ 检验、训练不平衡度衰减、SMOTE 插值伪影取整敏感性与
切分方差补测四项稳健性检验。结果表明：方法排序不存在跨模型跨口径的普适最优，LR 最优
SMOTE 而 XGBoost 最优类别权重，且排序稳定性高度依赖模型（选型分割间 Kendall τ 从
+1.0 到 −1.0）；全项目最高 Macro-F1（0.5436±0.0063）来自零重训练的阈值校准而非任何
训练期处理方法；特征筛选以主指标噪声内的代价换取 2.67 倍训练加速但无法压缩 XGBoost
体积；少数类召回对训练不平衡度本身高度敏感。漏检格局呈"类别 × 方法"强交互，DoS 仅在
XGBoost+SMOTE 下被有效挽救。全部结论可溯源至实验证据链并经三轮独立对抗审计。

**关键词**：网络入侵检测 类别不平衡 SMOTE 代价敏感学习 XGBoost

# Abstract

Network intrusion detection data are extremely imbalanced: attack samples are far outnumbered
by normal traffic, and attack categories are themselves highly skewed (the majority-to-minority
ratio reaches 431:1 in the UNSW-NB15 training split), causing conventional classifiers to
systematically miss minority-class attacks. Rather than proposing a new algorithm, this thesis
conducts a systematic comparison and lightweight improvement of class-imbalance handling
methods under a unified experimental protocol: 36 primary runs (3 models × 4 methods × 3
seeds) with all hyperparameters frozen before any results, leakage-prevention discipline
throughout, two extended controls — validation-set threshold calibration and gain-based
feature-selection lightweighting — and four robustness checks, including a preregistered
Kendall τ test of method-ranking consistency, imbalance-level decay analysis, a rounding
sensitivity test of SMOTE interpolation artifacts, and a per-seed resplit variance recheck
for deterministic configurations. Results show no cross-model, cross-metric winner: LR favors
SMOTE while XGBoost favors class weighting, and ranking stability is model-dependent
(τ from +1.0 to −1.0 across selection splits). The highest Macro-F1 of the entire study
(0.5436±0.0063) comes from zero-retraining threshold calibration rather than any training-time
method; feature selection buys a 2.67× training speedup at a within-noise Macro-F1 cost but
cannot shrink XGBoost size; minority recall is highly sensitive to the training imbalance
level itself. The missed-detection pattern is a strong class-by-method interaction, with DoS
effectively recovered only by XGBoost+SMOTE. All conclusions are traceable to an experimental
evidence chain and audited by three rounds of independent adversarial review.

**Keywords**: network intrusion detection, class imbalance, SMOTE, cost-sensitive learning, XGBoost
